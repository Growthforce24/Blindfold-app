Financial Terminology Glossary

Plain-English translations of standard accounting terms used in Blindfold.

COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)

The direct cost paid to acquire or manufacture the products sold to customers. Does not include rent or electricity.

Gross Profit Margin %

The proportion of money left over from sales revenue after subtracting item inventory costs. Formula: (Gross Profit / Sales) * 100

Net Profit

The real money remaining after subtracting ALL costs, inventory expenses, staff payroll, and operational overheads.

Accounts Receivable (Debtors)

Money owed to your business by customers who took goods on credit but haven't paid yet.

Depreciation

The gradual loss of value of long-term business equipment (e.g., computers, vehicles) over its useful lifespan.

Double-Entry Ledger

An accounting system where every entry has a matching debit and credit account, guaranteeing that your books stay perfectly balanced.
